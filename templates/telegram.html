Telegram

Aetheron, in a chat opens Tuesday

Three things: a bot you can buy from without leaving the conversation, a channel that posts what the protocol does on chain, and a group where people talk. This page is what each one is for, and what the bot can and cannot do, written before it ships rather than after.

The bot

Everything the website does, the bot does, because it is the same service underneath. It has no prices of its own and no allowances of its own. It asks the protocol and repeats the answer, which is the only way the number you are shown and the number you are charged can be guaranteed to match.

Linking a wallet

Before anything costs money, the bot needs to know which wallet it is acting for, and it will only accept one you can prove you own. You send your address, it gives you a one time message, you sign it in your wallet and paste the signature back.

That signature moves nothing and grants nothing. It signs no transaction and gives the bot no permission to spend. It exists because prices are worked out from the wallet: a previous mint holder pays half, and paying in AETH takes a further fifth off. If a chat could simply name any address, anyone could buy at half price on somebody else's entitlement and read their purchase history besides.
/link <wallet>

Start linking. The bot replies with a message to sign.

/wallet

Show which wallet this chat is linked to.

/unlink

Forget it. For a phone you are selling, or a chat you now share.

Trying it before paying

The free tier is the same three per wallet the site gives you, counted by the protocol rather than by the bot, so a second Telegram account gains you nothing. Both of these work in group chats, because watching an agent run in front of a room is the entire reason to be in one.

/example <component>

A real report from that component, in full, free.

/preview <agent>

Runs a store agent on live chain data for 25 seconds and sends back exactly what it printed. If it prints nothing because the chain is quiet, it says so rather than sending an empty message.

/agents

Which agents can be watched running.

Buying

You send a component and your input, the bot quotes the amount and the wallet to pay, you send it on chain and paste the transaction signature. It then runs the job and sends the file back into the chat. Any discount you are owed is already in the quote, because the price came from the protocol with your wallet attached.

/components

What can be run, and what each costs you specifically.

/buy <component> <input>

Quote it. Nothing is charged until you pay.

<paste a signature>

No command needed. You have just come back from your wallet with it on the clipboard, and asking for ceremony there is how a purchase gets abandoned.

<paste a contract address>

Offers to pull the holders, the authorities and the known risks on that token. In a private chat only, because a group where people paste addresses all day does not want an offer every time.

/pending

What you have waiting on payment, and what is running.

/cancel

Drop a quote you have not paid. Nothing was charged.

Getting things back

A file sent into a chat lives there until somebody clears a cache or changes phone. What you bought should not disappear with it, so everything is keyed to the wallet rather than the conversation. Link the same wallet on a new device and your history is there.

/assets

Everything this wallet has bought that produced a file.

/get <number>

Send one of them again.

What it will not do

Buying, your wallet and your files are private chat only. In a group the bot refuses and tells you why. A purchase flow in public shows everyone what you bought and what you paid, and that is not the bot's to share. It never asks for a private key or a seed phrase, and it never will: signing a message and handing over a key are not the same thing, and nothing Aetheron does needs the second one.

It also stays quiet. It answers commands, a pasted signature and a pasted contract address. It does not reply to ordinary conversation, and an unknown slash command in a group is ignored rather than answered, because it is usually meant for a different bot.

The channels

Announcements

Read only, and deliberately slow. Three things get posted and nothing else.

Every AETH burn, with the Solscan link so you can check it rather than take our word for it. A short note of what has been bought since the last one, counted by component and never by buyer. And the service going down or coming back, which is posted on the way down and on the way up and never in between.

A channel that says all systems operational every ten minutes trains people to ignore it, and then they ignore the one that matters.

Community

Open, and where the bot is most useful. Anyone can watch an agent run on live data or pull up a real example report without spending anything, in front of everybody, which is a far better answer to is this real than any thread we could write.

Buying happens in a private chat with the bot instead. Not because it is hidden, but because the flow would otherwise show the whole room what somebody bought and what they paid for it.

When

Tuesday. The bot and both channels open together, and the links appear here and on X the same day.

Everything on this page is what it does on the day it opens, not a list of intentions. It is written now rather than after so it can be held against what actually turns up.

Everything above already exists as endpoints the website uses, which is why the bot can be a way in rather than a second product. There is one protocol and one ledger. The chat is just another door into it.
