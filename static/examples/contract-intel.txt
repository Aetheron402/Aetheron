1. Verdict

Both the mint authority and freeze authority on this SPL mint are null, so no key can issue new supply or freeze holder accounts, which leaves market depth rather than contract control as the thing that determines whether it is safe to hold.

2. High Level Summary

This is the Bonk SPL token mint on Solana, owned by the standard SPL Token program (TokenkegQfeZyiNwAJbNbGKPFXCWuBvf9Ss623VQ5DA) and not itself executable. The parsed mint account reports mintAuthority null and freezeAuthority null with isInitialized true, decimals 5 and a supply of 8799457831408070266 raw units. That combination removes the two levers that normally let an issuer harm holders on Solana: there is no way to inflate supply and no way to freeze a token account. The residual risk is not in the contract, it is in the market and in what the scan could not resolve. Holder data did return: the largest single owner holds 8.17 percent, the top five 24.51 percent and the top ten 38.56 percent of supply across a 20 account sample.

3. Contract Identity and Role

A token mint account. parsed_type is mint, kind is token-mint, executable is false, and the owner_program is the standard SPL Token program, so this is a plain fungible token mint rather than a program or a token account. token_metadata names it Bonk with symbol Bonk, trading on Raydium. It has no custom program logic of its own: all behaviour is whatever the SPL Token program provides.

4. Technical Profile

On Solana an SPL mint carries only two privileged fields, and both are null here. mintAuthority null means the supply of 8799457831408070266 raw units at 5 decimals is fixed and cannot be increased by any signer. freezeAuthority null means no authority can immobilise a holder's associated token account. Because executable is false and the owner is the canonical SPL Token program, there is no upgradeable program, no proxy, no admin module and no upgrade authority attached to this asset. There is correspondingly no ABI to review and no custom function surface: that is expected for a bare SPL mint, not a gap. Power over holders through the mint itself is effectively zero; whatever governance or treasury activity exists around the project sits in separate accounts outside this scan.

6. Permission and Control Surface

• mintAuthority is null in parsed_info: supply is capped and no key can dilute holders.
• freezeAuthority is null in parsed_info: no key can freeze or immobilise a holder's token account, which is the usual Solana equivalent of a blacklist.
• executable is false and owner_program is the canonical SPL Token program: there is no upgrade authority and no custom admin code path attached to this mint.
• admin_risk and exploit_surface are both null in the scan, which is consistent with a bare mint that has no privileged surface to enumerate rather than evidence of a hidden one.

7. Risk Assessment

Overall Risk Score: 4/10
Centralization Score: 4/10
Data Quality Score: 7/10
Data Completeness Score: 9/10

• Centralization risk from the contract itself is minimal because both authority fields are null. There is no key to trust and no renouncement to verify: the fields are simply absent from the mint account.
• Monetary risk is bounded by mintAuthority being null. The supply figure of 8799457831408070266 raw units at 5 decimals is what exists and cannot grow.
• Technical risk is low in the sense that there is no custom program, but that also means nothing about the asset is verifiable by code review: the safety here rests entirely on the two null authority fields and on the SPL Token program's known behaviour.
• Honeypot risk is unassessed. honeypot_intel is null, so no buy or sell simulation was performed. With freezeAuthority null there is no mint-level mechanism to block a sale, but that reasoning is inference from the mint account, not a completed simulation.
• Distribution is moderately concentrated at the top of the measured sample: 38.56 percent across ten owners. Whether that represents custodial pools or individual holders is not resolvable from this scan, and the sample covers 20 accounts rather than the full holder set.
• The liquidity to valuation ratio is the practical exposure. A 274,668,528 market cap sitting above 149,971.27 of indexed depth on one Raydium pair means exit pricing, not contract permissions, is the dominant hazard for anyone sizing a position off the headline valuation.
